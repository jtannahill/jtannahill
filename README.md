## Why I replaced QuickBooks

In October 2026 I moved 1ness Strategies off QuickBooks Online to [Bigcapital](https://github.com/bigcapitalhq/bigcapital), a self-hosted open-source ledger, with my own billing and AI assistant on top.

- **QuickBooks rents you your own books.** The price keeps rising, and the export is report spreadsheets, not a database.
- **It held our cash.** In November 2025 QuickBooks Payments froze client deposits; it took two days of calls to release them.
- **Now I own it.** About $8 a month for the server, nightly encrypted backups, invoices drafted from each client's Ads spend, and an AI that only suggests until a person approves.

The catch: backups and security are my job, and I still use an accountant for taxes.
